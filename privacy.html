Privacy notice for the Ordnaro survey

Ordnaro is currently being founded. See the address above.

2. What is this about?

Ordnaro is a software product in development. With a short online survey and personal interview requests we want to learn which software companies use today, what bothers them and what a better solution would be worth to them.

3. Which data do we process?

  • Your answers in the form (e.g. industry, company size, systems used, effort, price expectations).
  • Optional: an e-mail address, a preferred time slot and your time zone, if you would like a call.
  • Optional: a reference code from your personal link. The code contains no name. In Edwin's contact list, however, it is matched to a company, so that answers and the launch offer can be linked to that company. It is therefore pseudonymous personal data.

We do not ask for your name or phone number. Taking part is voluntary. If you do not take part, there are no disadvantages for you.

4. What do we use the data for?

  • Analysing which software companies use, what bothers them and what they would pay.
  • Contacting you to arrange the 30-minute call, if you wish.
  • Noting your launch offer.

There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

5. Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR or UK GDPR), given via the checkbox in the form. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. Processing carried out before you withdraw remains lawful.

6. Google as technical service

The form and the answers are stored with Google (Google Forms and Google Sheets) in a private Gmail account.

  • For private Google accounts there is no data processing agreement as there is with Google Workspace. Google processes the data under its own terms and its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • A transfer to the USA is possible.
  • When you open the form, Google may collect its own technical data (e.g. IP address).

Ordnaro does not use any trackers of its own. If you prefer not to use the form, you can send your answers by e-mail to edwinkraus.assistant@gmail.com.

7. Hosting of this page (GitHub Pages)

This survey page is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open the page, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the address requested and information about your browser and operating system, and stores it in server logs in order to deliver the page and protect it against misuse. A transfer to the USA is possible.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the page securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information in GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

The page itself sets no cookies, uses no trackers and loads no content from other providers. Your answers are only sent directly to Google Forms when you submit (see section 6) and are not stored by GitHub.

8. Who receives the data?

Nobody except Edwin Kraus and Google as technical service. No disclosure to third parties, no sale. As host of this page, GitHub only receives the technical access data described in section 7, not your answers.

9. How long do we keep the data?

  • Answers: until 31.05.2027, then deleted.
  • Note of the launch offer (code or e-mail and the note "launch offer"): until 30.06.2027, then deleted.

If you withdraw your consent, we delete your data earlier.

  • data portability,

The competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg, Germany (https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de). If you are in the United Kingdom, you can contact the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office, https://ico.org.uk).
